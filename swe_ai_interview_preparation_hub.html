1. Strategy

For senior roles, the evaluation shifts from purely algorithmic "grinding" to demonstrating architectural judgment, leadership, resolving conflicts, and managing trade-offs. Prepare for these simultaneously as you apply.

5. Big Tech Nuances

Meta

Speed & Clean Code

Expect intense pacing in DSA (2 questions in 45 min). Code must run bug-free on the first try. Behavioral is called the "Jedi" round focusing heavily on conflict resolution and taking ownership.

Google

Fundamentals & Scale

Deep CS fundamentals. Problems might not map directly to LeetCode patterns. "Googliness" round checks for leadership, thriving in ambiguity, and doing the right thing. Very strict hiring committee.

Microsoft

Practicality & Architecture

System design is highly practical. Interviewers dig deep into past technical decisions, asking "Why did you build it that way?". Process can vary significantly by team.

Atlassian

Values & Collaboration

Rejects "algorithm gymnastics". Expect practical coding exercises and system designs. Values interviews are heavily weighted (Open company, no b.s., play as a team). You will fail if you don't align with their culture.

Salesforce

Enterprise & Domain

Focuses on large-scale B2B multi-tenant architectures. Expect a mix of standard coding, deep system design, and domain-specific questions depending on the cloud (Security, Data, Core).
